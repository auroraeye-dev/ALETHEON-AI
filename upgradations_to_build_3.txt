On the AlphaFold idea (#10) — straight talk
AlphaFold predicts protein 3D structure from sequence. For your tool — which summarizes clinical evidence about marketed drugs — AlphaFold has almost no use. It would be relevant if you pivoted to early-stage drug discovery (designing molecules against a target protein), which is a completely different product, market, and competitor set (Schrödinger, Isomorphic Labs, Recursion). I'd cut it from this list unless you're seriously considering that pivot. Adding AlphaFold integration "because it's cool" is the kind of feature that signals lack of focus to a YC partner.
On the NCBI Gene Bank / genomics idea (#9) — same caveat I gave you the first time. Genomics data is huge and different domain. The focused version that fits a drug tool is pharmacogenomics (PharmGKB) — gene-drug interactions, which I included above. That's the right slice; "NCBI Gene Bank correlation" without a specific use case is feature-creep.
